Start here

This pack teaches internal audits, CAPA and action items. It starts with what happens in a real company and only then shows the product. You do not need to know quality management or ISO standards to read it.

Three levels

Level What it covers Chapters For
1 What an audit and a CAPA are in real life, and who does what 01 to 04 everyone, including sales and management
2 How the product does each step, screen by screen 05 to 14 users, service, business analysts

Read Level 1 first, even if you only need the product. The screens make sense once you know the story behind them. Developers carry on after Level 3 into the technical documentation of Audit, CAPA and Action Items.

One story runs through the pack

Every chapter follows the same company. Cedar Valley Components makes precision metal parts, mostly pump housings and valve bodies. It has about 350 people and two plants, Riverside and Hillcrest. It holds an ISO 9001 certificate.

In this pack, Cedar Valley audits how it calibrates its measuring equipment. The audit finds a serious problem with overdue gauges, a smaller one with an old work instruction, and a weak spot in how records are kept. The rest of the pack follows each of them to the end.

How words are used

  • Every special word is explained in the glossary. The twelve words at the top of the glossary are enough to follow the story.
  • When a sentence points at something on screen, the screen label is in bold, for example Create Audit Program.
  • ISO terms appear in the "For ISO readers" boxes and in the glossary. The rest of the text uses plain words.
