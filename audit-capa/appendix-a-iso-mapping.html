Appendix A: ISO mapping

This appendix maps each step of the pack to the ISO 9001 clause that asks for it, and to the screen that does it. It is for readers who know the standard, such as quality managers preparing for a certification audit.

The clause wording is paraphrased, not quoted, because the standards are copyrighted. The sub-clause letters follow ISO 9001:2015 and must be checked against a licensed copy before this page is published.

Preventive action has no clause of its own in ISO 9001:2015. The standard handles prevention through risk-based thinking, clause 6.1, and through the check for similar problems in 10.2. The pack still uses the word, because the product and many companies do.

ISO 9001:2026

ISO 9001:2026 was published in September 2026, according to the certification bodies DQS, BSI and DNV. It is a targeted update. None of them reports a change to the internal audit requirements in 9.2 or to corrective action in 10.2. The old clauses 10.1 and 10.3 merge into a new 10.1 on continual improvement. Certificates to ISO 9001:2015 stay valid during a three-year transition.

The mapping above therefore holds for both editions. Check the clause numbers in clause 10 against the 2026 text before quoting them.

ISO 19011

ISO 19011 is guidance on how to audit, not a requirement. Its steps for a single audit are initiate, prepare, conduct, report and follow up. Chapters 08 to 11 follow them. Its "audit programme" means all audits planned for a period. One audit program in this pack is narrower. It defines one recurring audit.

Other standards, in short

ISO 13485, medical devices

Internal audit is clause 8.2.4, corrective action 8.5.2, and preventive action keeps its own clause, 8.5.3. Corrective action must be in proportion to the risk. Record keeping is stricter and tied to the life of the device.

21 CFR Part 820, United States medical devices

Since 2 February 2026, Part 820, now called the Quality Management System Regulation, requires a quality system that complies with ISO 13485, plus some FDA additions. The old audit and CAPA sections of Part 820 are gone. The duties now come through ISO 13485.

AS9100, aerospace

AS9100 builds on ISO 9001 and adds aerospace topics such as product safety and counterfeit parts. For corrective action, secondary sources say it asks companies to consider human factors and to update risks when they close an action. Aerospace customers often add their own CAPA rules on top.
