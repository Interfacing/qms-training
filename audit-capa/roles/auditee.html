Role card: auditee

For the people whose work is audited. At Cedar Valley, Grace, the production supervisor, is the auditee for machining.

What you do

  • Prepare: read what the auditors will check and have the records ready.
  • On the day, show how the work is really done, and answer questions.
  • Afterwards, carry out the fixes that are given to you.

Where your tasks arrive

You get no audit tasks, only emails. An engagement letter comes when the audit is scheduled, and an "Upcoming Audit" reminder before it. Action items you own arrive in To Do as Execute the Action.

Screens you use

  • To Do and the action item task.

Chapters 01, 02 and 13.

What good looks like

  • The auditors see the work as it is done every day, not a show.
  • Findings are agreed as facts, and fixes are done by their due date.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the audit as a test to pass. Hidden problems stay unfixed.
  • Missing the emails, because there is no task.
