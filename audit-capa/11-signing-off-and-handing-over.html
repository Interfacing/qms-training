Signing off and handing findings over

The audit ends with one task for the audit owner. Marco reads the report, sends each finding that needs work to the right place, and signs off. After this, the fixes run on their own.

Read the report

Once Sam submits the results, the audit is Marco's again. He reads the findings and decides what happens to each.

Marco gets Review And Approve Audit Results in To Do. The form is headed Review audit report. It shows the requirements and findings, and ends with the section Escalation.

Hand the findings over

The overdue gauges need a CAPA. The old work instruction needs a quality event. Elena, the quality manager, will review both. The observation stays in the report.

Open Trigger Quality Event(s) / CAPA(s) under Escalation.

  1. QE / CAPA Reviewer: who reviews what you create. Pick this first.
  2. Finding(s) for Quality Event offers every finding not yet handed over. Pick, then click Create Quality Event.
  3. Finding(s) for CAPA offers only major findings. Pick, then click Create CAPA.
  4. In Task Resolution, Sign-Off closes the audit. Clarification Required asks for more information before sign-off.

Findings picked together become one CAPA or one quality event. To get one record per finding, pick and create them one at a time. A finding can be handed over once, to a CAPA or to a quality event, not both.

Distribute audit report to sends the report by email at sign-off.

If the finding lists stay empty

On the demo system, both finding lists stayed empty, and the audit was signed off with nothing created. Elena opened the CAPA and the quality event by hand instead. The result is the same, with one extra step.

Open CAPA > New CAPA. Set Event Type to Audit Finding (Internal), pick the audit in Audit Plan ID (Internal), and attach the finding under Add Related Findings further down.

  1. Event Type: where the problem came from.
  2. CAPA Reviewer: Elena.
  3. RCA Owner: Ahmed, who will find the root cause.
  4. Eff. Check Responsible: Lina, who will check later that the fix worked.
  5. Audit Plan ID (Internal): the audit, AP-00058.

Submit creates the CAPA. Chapter 13 shows the same for a quality event.

Common mistakes

  • Signing off before handing the findings over. Check the finding lists first.
  • Picking several findings at once when each needs its own owner. They end up in one record.
  • Forgetting the reviewer. Without QE / CAPA Reviewer, nothing can be created.
